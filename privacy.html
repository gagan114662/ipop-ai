privacy

Privacy should be obvious.

ipop.ai is an early Mac app that helps you talk to AI, understand what is on your screen, and start useful agents. This page is a plain-English placeholder for launch day and should be replaced with counsel-reviewed legal terms before a broader release.

What ipop.ai may use

When you choose to use ipop.ai, the app may process your voice input, visible screen context, app names, prompts, agent output, and basic diagnostics needed to operate the product.

Control

Screen and microphone access should be permissioned by macOS and visible to you. ipop.ai should only use context needed to answer or act on the request you make.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to hello@ipop.ai.
