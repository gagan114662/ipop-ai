Privacy

Privacy policy

ipop.ai is a beta Mac app that lets you use voice, approved screen context, and AI providers to control your Mac and run background agent sessions. This policy explains the practical data flow for the launch beta.

Information the app may process

Depending on the task you ask for, ipop.ai may process your spoken request, the resulting transcript, visible screen context, app names, action results, agent output, local settings, diagnostics, and crash or error logs.

Voice and transcription

Dictation uses the speech provider configured in the app. The beta prefers AssemblyAI when configured and can fall back to Apple Speech. Those providers may receive audio or transcript data needed to perform speech recognition.

Screen context

macOS controls Screen Recording permission. When you make a screen-aware request, the app may send screenshots or extracted visible text to the AI provider selected on your Mac so it can answer or choose the right action.

AI providers and agent sessions

Early beta builds use providers you configure locally, such as CLI sessions or API keys. Prompts, transcripts, screen context, and agent outputs may be sent to those providers according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Mac control

App launches, typing, key presses, clicks, and scrolling are executed locally through macOS Accessibility permissions. ipop.ai does not give a remote server direct control of your desktop.

Website

The launch website offers a direct GitHub download and does not collect an email address through the download button. If you contact hello@ipop.ai, we use your message to reply and support the beta.

Retention

The app may store local settings, memory, logs, and session history on your Mac so the beta can function and be debugged. You can request deletion help by emailing hello@ipop.ai.

Your control

You can revoke microphone, Accessibility, and Screen Recording permissions in macOS System Settings. You can also remove local provider keys and uninstall the app at any time.

Contact

Questions, security reports, or deletion requests can be sent to hello@ipop.ai.
