Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-19

1. What we collect

When you contact iPOP or purchase a service, we collect the information you provide (such as name, email address, organisation, and the project context you share) plus standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamps). When you pay, Stripe collects and processes your payment details directly — iPOP does not see or store your card number.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, process checkout, deliver the purchased service, send transactional email (receipts, kickoff messages, status updates), maintain security records, and meet legal obligations.

3. Who receives it

Subprocessors used to operate iPOP include: Stripe (payments), Vercel (web hosting), Cloudflare (DNS and edge backend), and email infrastructure providers used to send transactional messages. Each subprocessor receives only the data needed to perform its function.

4. Retention

Project-related communications and records are retained for the duration of the engagement plus seven years for accounting and audit purposes. You can request earlier deletion of data not required to be retained by law.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Send requests to privacy@ipop.ai.

6. International transfers

Subprocessors may process data in jurisdictions other than your own. Where required, standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards apply.

7. Security

Access to customer data is restricted to authorised personnel. Transport is over TLS. Card data is not stored on iPOP infrastructure.

8. Contact

Reply to your Stripe receipt or contact privacy@ipop.ai for privacy questions, and security@ipop.ai for security reports.
