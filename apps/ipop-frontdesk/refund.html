Refund Policy

Effective: 2026-05-19

One-time service purchases

One-time service purchases (such as Lead Cleanup Sprint and Proof Pack Build) are eligible for a full refund within 24 hours of purchase, provided work has not yet started. Once intake or delivery work has begun, the unused portion may be refunded at iPOP's discretion based on work completed.

Subscriptions

Managed CRM Ops and any other monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal link in your receipt. Cancellation stops future renewals; the current paid period remains active until its end date. Partial-month refunds are not provided.

Failed delivery

If iPOP cannot deliver the purchased service within the timeline communicated at kickoff, you may request a full refund for the undelivered portion. We will respond within five business days.

How to request a refund

Reply to your Stripe receipt or email billing@ipop.ai with the original transaction reference and the reason for the request.

Chargebacks

Please contact iPOP before initiating a chargeback. Most disputes are resolved faster directly than through a card network.
